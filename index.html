Welcome In

A Local Place With Plenty To Come Back For.

Water Hole No. 1 is a family-owned bar and grill where a casual meal can turn into a full night out. Come hungry for burgers, chicken, appetizers and ribs, stay for a cold drink, and make a night of it when the music starts.

Whether you're stopping in for lunch, meeting friends after work, or looking for live entertainment on the weekend, you'll find a relaxed atmosphere and a menu built for sharing.

About Us

Built Around Food, Friends & Community.

Water Hole No. 1 is family-owned and operated in Winnemucca. Our goal is simple: serve good food, pour cold drinks and create a place where locals, travelers and families can relax and enjoy themselves.

We've made our home on East Winnemucca Boulevard with room for food, conversation, events and live music.
