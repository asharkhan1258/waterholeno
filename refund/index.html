Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains how order concerns, cancellations, and refunds may be handled.

Third-Party Orders

Online ordering from our website may be completed through DoorDash. Refunds, cancellations, credits, delivery issues, and platform charges for those orders may need to be requested through DoorDash and are subject to its applicable policies.

Incorrect or Missing Items

Report incorrect or missing items as soon as reasonably possible. For third-party orders, use the support process attached to that order. For direct orders, contact the restaurant promptly.

Food Quality Concerns

If there is a quality problem, contact us promptly with enough information to identify the order. Depending on the circumstances, an appropriate resolution may include replacement, credit, or refund where appropriate.

Cancellations

Food is often prepared shortly after an order is received. Direct-order cancellation requests should be made immediately and cannot be guaranteed after preparation begins. Third-party cancellations are governed by the platform used to place the order.

Delivery Issues

For third-party delivery delays, missing deliveries, or delivery-location problems, contact the applicable delivery platform through your order.

Change of Mind

Because restaurant food is prepared for individual orders and is perishable, refunds generally are not provided solely for a change of mind after preparation begins or food is provided, subject to applicable law.

Refund Method

Approved direct-order refunds are generally returned through an appropriate method based on the original transaction. Third-party platforms control the method and timing of refunds or credits they approve.
