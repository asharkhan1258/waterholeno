About Water Hole No. 1

A Comfortable Stop In The Heart Of Winnemucca.

Water Hole No. 1 is a family-owned bar and grill built around good food, cold drinks and a welcoming place to spend time with friends and family.

Our menu brings together burgers, chicken, appetizers, ribs, desserts and drinks, while live music and events add another reason to stay awhile.

Whether you're local or passing through, we'd love to have you at the table.
